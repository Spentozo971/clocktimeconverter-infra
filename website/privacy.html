Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ClockTime Converter ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use clocktimeconverter.example (the "Site"). We built this Site to be usable without creating an account or handing over personal data, and this policy reflects that approach.

1. Information we collect

The core tools on this Site — the world clock and time zone converter, and the unit converter — run entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. Your inputs, such as the cities you choose to compare or the values you convert, are processed locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers or stored by us.

We do collect limited technical information automatically when you visit the Site, including:

  • Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) for security and diagnostic purposes
  • Aggregated, anonymized analytics about which pages are visited and general traffic patterns

If you submit the contact form, we collect the information you provide — your name, email address, and message — solely to respond to your inquiry.

2. How we use information

Any information we do collect is used only to operate, maintain, and improve the Site, to respond to inquiries submitted through the contact form, and to detect and prevent abuse or security issues. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your contact form submissions for marketing without your separate consent.

3. Cookies and local storage

The Site may use minimal cookies or browser local storage to remember display preferences, such as your preferred clock format or previously selected cities. These are not used to track you across other websites.

4. Third-party services

We may use third-party services for hosting, web fonts, and basic analytics. These providers may process limited technical data (such as IP address) as part of delivering their service, subject to their own privacy policies. We do not use third-party advertising networks on this Site.

5. Data retention

Server logs are retained for a limited period for security and troubleshooting purposes and then deleted or anonymized. Contact form submissions are retained only as long as needed to address your inquiry, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Since the core tools on this Site don't require us to collect personal data, most of these rights apply primarily to contact form submissions. To make a request, use the details on our Contact page.

7. Children's privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the Site after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out via our Contact page or email hello@clocktimeconverter.example.
